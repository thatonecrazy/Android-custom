Photo wallpapers bundled with Veneer
=====================================

All three are from Wikimedia Commons. Veneer crops them to phone size and recolors them
(red petals kept, everything else darkened; or a soft lavender tint).

Spider lilies
  "Lycoris Radiata 2017 2" by Chihiro H
  https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Lycoris_Radiata_2017_2_(228827637).jpeg
  License: CC BY 3.0, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0
  Changes: cropped, background darkened, reds deepened.

Lone lily
  "Red spider lily (Lycoris radiata)" by Offnfopt
  https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Red_spider_lily_(Lycoris_radiata).webp
  License: public domain.
  Changes: cropped, background darkened, placed on black.

Mirror sky
  "Uyuni Salt Lake (Salar de Uyuni), Bolivia, 12-2022" by Abel Maestro Garcia
  https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Uyuni_Salt_Lake_(Salar_de_Uyuni),_Bolivia,_12-2022.jpg
  License: public domain.
  Changes: cropped to portrait, sharpened, lavender tint.
